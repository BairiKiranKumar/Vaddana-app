# 5 Questions Before You Pay for a Business Website
*By @kiranbuildswithai — a practical free checklist*

### 1. Who is actually visiting?
Not “everyone.” Describe the one person most likely to enquire, and the situation that led them here.

### 2. What must they understand in the first ten seconds?
Write a plain sentence describing what you offer, who it is for, and where you operate. Avoid “redefining excellence.”

### 3. What makes you credible?
Choose real photos, honest pricing information, certifications when relevant, processes, or permissioned customer feedback. Don't fake trust indicators.

### 4. What's the one next action?
Call, WhatsApp, book, buy, or visit. Pick a primary action; make it prominent, accessible and mobile-friendly.

### 5. What would make your website unmistakably yours?
Think about a real product, place, material, teaching method, or customer experience. A cool 3D animation alone is not an answer.

**Bonus test:** Open the design on an ordinary phone with video disabled. Can someone still understand the business and contact you? If yes, you have a foundation worth decorating.
