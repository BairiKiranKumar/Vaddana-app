## Editorial rule: proof first, framework second, promotion third
- **Reveal:** The final mobile + desktop result within the first seconds (test, don't assume universal algorithm rules).
- **Decode:** One visual idea or interaction, its audience/job, and the engineering choice.
- **Demonstrate:** A genuine before/after, prototype, source note, or user-test quote (consent).
- **Trade-off:** Show what you removed, mobile/reduced-motion handling, file size or real device result.
- **Invite:** One specific, low-friction request, not four CTAs: 'Reply BRIEF for the research checklist' or 'DM your category for an idea.'
- **Credit:** Name original studios in breakdowns. Rebuild a *technique* in original design, not their client assets, logos or layout.

## Track qualified outcomes, not vanity metrics only
- For each post record: format, hook, credited source, reach, watch retention (where available), saves, sends, profile visits, bio/link taps, qualified DMs, discovery calls, projects secured.
- **Do not claim causation** after one viral post. Compare topics and creative variants over several posts.
- Add one brief question for enquiries: “Which post made you reach out?” Record the answer voluntarily.
- When possible, keep spec portfolio site with a conversion route to your services and public case study.

## Rights and client privacy
Written permission is required for publishing private business files, unpublished site captures, private analytics, customer interviews and recognizable client photos. Studio breakdowns should link/credit creators and use limited commentary/reference, not redistribute others' complete code/visual assets. Label concept work `Spec concept — not affiliated with the business` prominently.
