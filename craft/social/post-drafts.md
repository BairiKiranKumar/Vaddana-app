# Social Drafts — adapt with your actual screenshots, links and measured evidence
**Never publish a metric without a real measurement, or use demo images as documentary proof.** Each sample below is intentionally specific rather than vague “AI changed everything” marketing.

## Threads 01 — the main thesis
Most AI websites don't look generic because AI can't code.

They look generic because the brief contains no actual business truth.

What changes the result for me:
1. Find what real customers ask.
2. Map every section to one of those questions.
3. Build a visual idea from the product, people or space.
4. Prototype one signature interaction.
5. Remove everything that slows the visitor down.

The AI handles implementation faster. It doesn't decide what makes the business different.

I’m testing this process through real and clearly labeled spec websites at @kiranbuildswithai.

## Threads 02 — study a studio, credit openly
I studied Immersive Garden's *Cartier in Time* project.

What stood out wasn't the animation quality alone. The interface uses the structure of a clock to navigate twelve films.

That's the lesson: **a navigation pattern can come from the subject itself.**

For a local brand, I'm not trying to copy the Cartier visuals. I'm asking what structure already exists in the product that could become the interaction.

Original studio / case: https://immersive-g.com/projects/cartier-in-time/

## Threads 03 — discipline vs spectacle
My new test for a “premium” landing page:

Turn off the background video. Disable scroll animation. Switch to a small Android screen.

Is the offer still clear? Can I find proof? Can I complete the main action?

If not, the effects are doing too much and the design isn't doing enough.

I'm now designing the fallback alongside the signature animation rather than after it.

## Instagram Reel 01 — 20–30 seconds, original build
**0–2s**: Show the final mobile homepage with the signature scene. On-screen: “A premium [niche] website. No generic AI template.”
**2–7s**: Show source material (real approved packaging, signage, physical textures). “The idea came from the business.”
**7–13s**: Show direction A/B/C in quick shots. “Three directions. One chosen.”
**13–22s**: Before → after of ONE interaction (no code wall).
**22–30s**: Show mobile action working. “Beauty is useless if customers can't enquire.”
**Caption:** A concept doesn't start with “make it modern.” It starts with [one true observation]. Built with [actual tools]. [Real/Spec label]. For an original website concept, DM your business category. #webdesign #creativecoding

## Instagram Carousel 01 — “five checks before you call it premium”
1. Cover: Your website looks expensive. But does it work?
2. Understand the offer without a 4-second intro.
3. Prove it's this business, not any competitor.
4. Put the main action where a thumb can find it.
5. Make it useful without animations.
6. Measure it on a real phone.
7. CTA: Save this checklist. I'm sharing the real briefs and build breakdowns at @kiranbuildswithai.

## Instagram Reel 02 — decode / rebuild ethically
**Opening:** split-frame: source case-study screenshot (brief, credited) vs your own original prototype.
**Script:** “Immersive Garden made time the navigation for Cartier. I'm borrowing the thinking, not the design. For a [different brand] with [verified meaningful stages], that means [your original layout/interaction]. The same principle, completely different execution.”
**Ending:** show what changes on mobile/reduced motion. Put original source URL in caption, no implication of affiliation.
