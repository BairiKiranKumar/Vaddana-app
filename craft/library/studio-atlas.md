## Studio learning cards
### 1. Eames Institute / Instrument
**Observed:** Team visited the Eames Ranch; identified a 'thoughtful host' organizing principle; built a maintainable CMS with a scalable page and component system, and reported testing all components to WCAG 2.1 AA. **Extract:** physical immersion → visitor mindset → consistent interaction philosophy → flexible CMS. **Local experiment:** A salon could use real stylist tools, textures and an expert guide, *not* random luxury-stock imagery. **Source:** [case](https://www.instrument.com/work/eames-institute/).

### 2. Cartier In Time / Immersive Garden
**Observed:** A twelve-hour clock-inspired carousel organizes twelve films. **Extract:** navigation follows subject matter. **Local experiment:** For a premium coffee brand, organize flavors around *real* bean processing stages, not an arbitrary horizontal gallery. **Source:** [case](https://immersive-g.com/projects/cartier-in-time/).

### 3. Webby Awards / BASIC/DEPT
**Observed:** User research exposed problems with category discoverability; solution kept media types/category options visible in a redesigned winners gallery. **Extract:** reduce information friction before adding polish. **Local experiment:** A preschool must make age groups, timings, location, fees enquiry and visit booking discoverable. **Source:** [case](https://www.basicagency.com/case-studies/the-webby-awards-website-design).

### 4. Structured / Locomotive
**Observed:** Transformation from passive Bitcoin to purposeful asset becomes a conceptual throughline, supported by refined typographic and motion decisions. **Extract:** write a concept sentence strong enough to guide images, transitions and words. **Local experiment:** A pickle brand's packaging texture and preparation story could anchor a visual system. **Source:** [case](https://locomotive.ca/en/work/structured).

### 5. Pinterest Business / Instrument
**Observed:** Workshops and three rounds of research/testing narrowed who the site should serve; content balanced education with real-world examples. **Extract:** site hierarchy is an audience-priority decision. **Local experiment:** A dentist site may split nervous first-time patients from returning patients, with distinct paths. **Source:** [case](https://www.instrument.com/work/pinterest-business).

## Weekly studio-research protocol (30–60 minutes)
1. Choose a **specific** project from a studio's *own* case study, not just a visual gallery.
2. Document the stated client challenge, audience, insight, creative idea, and technical approach. Separate facts from interpretation.
3. Record desktop AND mobile if accessible. Ask: first visible claim? first action? skipped animation? image weight? reduced motion? keyboard behavior?
4. Reconstruct ONE mechanism in an isolated toy prototype; do not replicate protected layouts or assets.
5. Write a three-line lesson: what worked, where it would fail, and one local-business application.
6. Save to `craft/library/inspirations/<slug>.md` using the decomposition outline in `craft/library/motion-scroll-3d.md`.
