## A. Discovery: 45-minute owner conversation
Ask open questions. Follow up with a recent concrete example instead of accepting generic positioning claims.
1. What are your three most important services/products, and which is most profitable or most important to grow?
2. Why do customers pick you rather than the shop next door? Tell me about the last three customers.
3. What do people misunderstand before calling? What do you explain on WhatsApp every day?
4. Which customer group should we attract first? Who is NOT a good fit?
5. What objections do customers raise: price, safety, ingredients, location, time, trust, fit, staff?
6. What proof can we show: real product, interior, reviews with permission, process, credentials, team?
7. What is the current path from discovery to paid customer? Which step loses them?
8. What must the site do: WhatsApp, enquiries, booking, store, admission, app login? Rank one primary action.
9. Are pricing, availability, hours, service areas, legal policies and guarantees approved for publication?
10. Which brand materials exist: logo source, photography, packaging, palette, signage, typography rights, video?
11. Which competitor feels similar? Which competitor is disappointing and why?
12. Who approves copy/visuals? Who owns domain, hosting, security and customer-data handling?

## B. Customer conversations (3–5 exploratory conversations, or as feasible)
**Recruit people who actually resemble the site's intended visitors; obtain consent.** Do not treat a small sample as representative statistics.
1. Tell me about the last time you tried to find a [category] provider/product.
2. What first made you trust or distrust a business in that category?
3. What were you comparing? What almost made you quit?
4. Which information did you need before contacting someone?
5. How would you describe your choice to a friend? Capture their language verbatim with permission.
6. Show me how you would book/enquire/order on this existing website. Where would you expect to click?
7. What would have saved you one phone call?

## F. Prototype test script (recommended small qualitative round)
**Task, not leading instruction:** “You want to book a visit for a child aged [valid age]. Starting here, show me what you would do.”
- [ ] Participant understands offer unaided within 10 seconds (observation, not universal target).
- [ ] Can locate actual offering, location, opening hours and enquiry path.
- [ ] Can complete task with own language; log hesitation, misclick and follow-up.
- [ ] Test mobile first; repeat after improvement. Five is NN/g's common heuristic for a round of qualitative tests, **not** for quantitative significance: https://www.nngroup.com/articles/how-many-test-users/.
